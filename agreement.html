1. Scope of Work

Services are provided based on the specific project scale, architectural complexity, and diagnostic results. Operational scope vectors may alter dynamically depending on deep technical findings during a session.

Financial quotes or repair windows are rough assessments and are not final or binding until complete hardware evaluations or diagnostics are finished.

Any technical support, administration, or physical configurations requested outside the baseline parameters of the original ticket will require updated fees or secondary scheduling.

Upfront retainer transactions or flat service fees are required before diagnostic deployment runs, consulting hours, or remote sessions begin.
