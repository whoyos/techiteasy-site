Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, technical device details, configuration metrics, service inquiry parameters, and encrypted third-party payment confirmation flags.

2. How We Use Information

To safely initialize remote diagnostic tunnels, deliver system logs, process credit profiles securely, improve overall engineering quality, and maintain system firewalls.

3. Information Sharing

We do not lease, trade, or sell client datasets. Data points are strictly routed directly into compliant processing environments (such as Stripe), calendar booking managers (Calendly), or localized law authorities when formally requested by law.

4. Data Security

We run comprehensive encryption keys and rigid access controls across our internal communication paths. However, no data transmission network over the internet is completely immune to security risks.

5. Cookies

Our website utilizes light analytics cookies to monitor diagnostic workflow navigation steps, maintain baseline loading speeds, and manage user interactions safely.

6. Third‑Party Services

We leverage services provided by Stripe, Calendly, premium static hosting systems, and encrypted email services. Their independent protection frameworks operate entirely outside our administrative governance.

7. Updates

We reserve all rights to alter our data handling blueprints at any given time to stay aligned with evolving global data protection guidelines.
